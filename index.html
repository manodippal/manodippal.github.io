01 · About

Research with a purpose.

My research aims to connect materials chemistry with practical electrochemical devices for sustainable energy conversion.

I am a PhD Scholar at the Indian Institute of Technology Bombay working on advanced nanostructured electrocatalysts and electrochemical energy conversion. My research focuses on industrial-scale alkaline and seawater electrolysis, hybrid electrolysis and solar-driven green hydrogen production.

My approach combines interface engineering, electronic-structure modulation and renewable-energy integration to bridge catalyst-level advances with practical electrolyzer technologies.

02 · Research

From catalyst to electrolyzer.

A research portfolio spanning fundamental electrochemistry, catalyst reconstruction and device-level hydrogen generation.

01 · HYDROGEN

HER / OER & Water Splitting

Advanced transition-metal-based electrocatalysts for efficient hydrogen and oxygen evolution under alkaline conditions and at practically relevant current densities.

02 · SEAWATER

Hybrid Seawater Electrolysis

Developing alternative anodic chemistry and AEM electrolyzer architectures to reduce the energy and selectivity challenges associated with conventional seawater splitting.

03 · CARBON

Electrochemical CO₂ Conversion

Exploring catalyst and electrolyzer strategies for converting CO₂ into value-added chemicals and fuels using renewable electricity.

04 · OXYGEN

Oxygen Reduction Reaction

Electrocatalytic ORR studies using rotating disk and rotating ring-disk methodologies to understand activity, kinetics and reaction pathways.

05 · MATERIALS

Nanostructured Electrocatalysts

Designing heterostructures, layered hydroxides, phosphides, sulfides, doped materials and carbon-supported catalysts with controlled interfaces.

06 · DEVICES

Electrolyzer Engineering

Moving beyond half-cell metrics toward membrane-electrode assemblies, stability testing, gas evolution and system-level performance.

03 · Selected Projects

Selected research projects.

Hybrid seawater · SOR

Nb-doped MoS₂ for hybrid seawater splitting

Investigating Nb-doped MoS₂ electrocatalysts for sulfide oxidation reaction (SOR)-assisted hydrogen production in alkaline media, with emphasis on polysulfide chemistry and sulfur recovery.

AEM electrolyzer · seawater

Ni₂P@NiCoLDH-o-CNT

Heterostructured nickel phosphide / NiCo layered double hydroxide supported on mildly oxidized carbon nanotubes for alkaline seawater electrolysis, including long-duration operation and solar-assisted hydrogen generation.

ORR · electrochemistry

Electrocatalytic oxygen reduction

Mechanistic and kinetic evaluation of ORR using RDE/RRDE methods, including peroxide pathway analysis, Tafel behaviour and electrochemical surface-area considerations.

Water oxidation · interfaces

Plasma-engineered phosphide heterostructures

Studying reconstruction of Ni₂P/Fe₂P-type heterostructures into catalytically active oxyhydroxide phases for water oxidation at high current density.
